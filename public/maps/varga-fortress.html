Players' Map — Varga's Fortress

Hand-drawn flimsi. Smudged from being slept on. Drawn by Warrick Raden from memory while he was waiting for somebody to find him in the filtration plant. Republic-era ruin retrofitted into a Hutt court — three floors above ground, two below. The dungeons are below. The throne room is on the ground floor. The guest quarters and the gambling halls are above. Hover any labeled area for Raden's notes.

Raden's Briefing

"Old stone fortress on the far shore of Nymeve Lake. Republic-era ruin retrofitted into a Hutt court — moss on the walls, blaster turrets in the moss, gaudy banners over the turrets. Three floors above ground, two below. "I drew these from memory while I was waiting for somebody to find me. They're not perfect. The dungeon corridor on the lower right may have been re-walled since I last went down there — Varga rebuilds the cells when he's bored. But the throne room layout, the guard posts, the kitchen approach, the aviary — those are accurate. "Two ways in if you can't use the dock. A sewer outflow on the lake side — narrow, but it skips the gate. And a kitchen delivery hatch on the south wall that the Gamorreans don't watch because nobody respects the kitchen. I'd take the dock. Bring entertainment papers. Look like you belong."

The Three Approaches

Front Door — The Dock

Walk in as guests. Entertainment papers, cover identities, audition before Varga. Highest visibility, fastest path to the throne room, best access to court intel — but every step is observed. Raden's recommended approach.

Side Door — Sewer Outflow

Wade in through the lake-side outflow tunnel. Comes up inside the lower service runs beneath the kitchens. Bypasses the dock and the audition entirely, but puts the crew in the bowels of the fortress with no cover and no excuse if found.

Back Door — Kitchen Delivery Hatch

Knock at the south wall hatch with the supply rhythm. The kitchen master opens it without questions because the Gamorreans don't watch the kitchen. Lands the crew in the staff workflow, where they can move freely between levels — if they look like they belong on staff.

The Layout

Throne Room (Ground Floor)

Varga's court. Hover-sled throne, TC-663 at his side, the audition, the public spectacles. The center of every social scene in the court phase.

Gambling Halls (Upstairs)

Sabacc tables, guest quarters, private rooms for high rollers. Where the crew will be quartered if they pass the audition, and where most court-political scenes happen.

Aviary (East Tower)

Converted Republic observation tower. Vesper-hawks and Corellian striker-eagles. Narrow catwalks, openable cages. The worst place in the fortress to be ambushed.

Kitchens & South Wall

Kitchen, prep, cold storage, and the south wall delivery hatch. The unwatched artery of the fortress. Staff move freely between levels.

East Tower & Stairs

The vertical spine. Stairs up to the aviary and gambling halls, stairs down to the kitchens and the dungeon corridor. Guard post at the base.

Dungeon Corridor (Lower Right)

Descent into the old Neimoidian detention block — sealed blast doors, silent guard droids. Raden warns the layout below may have been re-walled. Treat as suggestion only.
