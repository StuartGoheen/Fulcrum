I watched the *Banshee* set down at Blackwind Point. The local enforcers, Kessra and her Vipers, were waiting. Reyla Sun, as captain, made the call to pay the landing tax, a bribe, to clear the dock. It was a pragmatic choice; it bought them immediate passage without violence, saving time and resources. I noted that Reyla herself seemed to weigh the cost of that expediency later, observing that she did not love what they became to make it cheap. The alternative, a confrontation, might have established a different kind of presence, but it would have drawn more attention and risked the mission before it even began. A choice for quiet efficiency over a display of force.

Their subsequent search for Warrick Raden led them through the encampment, a slow process, before they located him at the filtration plant. Once Raden was found, I marked another significant choice: they decided to cut him loose. This move, too, bought them something — freedom from an immediate entanglement, a potential liability, and perhaps a clearer path to their primary objective. Yet, it clearly came at an internal cost, as I observed the crew arguing for a full hour afterward. It revealed a split in their approach, some valuing the clean break, others perhaps seeing the missed opportunity for a stronger alliance or a different kind of leverage. Such divisions often surface when the path ahead is unclear, and the easy way out is rarely without its own complexities.

The situation with Raden is still unfolding. I watched Trandoshan mercenaries engage them, and Raden's sentry droid was quickly destroyed. The crew is now actively defending him, a reversal of their earlier decision to cut him loose, forced by circumstances. I also made a note of Old Pike’s observation about a voice in the corridor, a detail that might be nothing, or it might be a thread. It is often the small, unplaced things that bear watching. I am curious to see if this shift in their approach to Raden — from cutting him loose to defending him — will solidify into a different pattern of behavior, or if it is merely a reactive measure to a threat they did not anticipate.
