I observed their arrival on Endor, the Forest Moon. The landing itself was uneventful, a quiet descent. Varth gave his usual briefing, and Maya, as always, kept the ship steady. Master Denia remained mostly withdrawn. The first choice I marked was at the dock. Vex and Hara decided to pay the bribe. It cleared their path quickly, no fuss, no complications. A smooth start, certainly. But Vex’s later words about not loving what they ‘became’ to make it cheap tell a different story. It bought them ease, but perhaps at a cost to their own sense of themselves. Another crew might have pushed back, challenged the demand, risked a confrontation for principle or simply out of defiance. This one chose the path of least immediate resistance, a pragmatic calculation.

Later, I watched them deal with the target. They chose to ‘cut them loose.’ This was a decision to limit their own exposure, to shed a potential liability before it complicated their primary objective. Effective, in that sense. It brought a swift end to that particular entanglement. Yet, I noted the long argument that followed among them, particularly with Maya. It seemed to weigh on her. Such a choice, while efficient, leaves questions hanging. It sacrifices a potential future connection, or even just a clean conscience, for immediate security. Someone else might have seen more value in establishing a different kind of trust, or ensuring a more complete resolution.

The forest itself quickly presented its challenges. I saw the encounter with the boar-wolf, a brief, sharp reminder of the world they had entered. Now, the Ewok war party has them. The canopy ambush is still unfolding, the outcome not yet clear. I am watching to see how they navigate this new contact. Will it be a parley, a careful negotiation that seeks understanding? Or will it escalate to a more direct, violent confrontation? The path they choose will reveal more about their instincts, their willingness to engage with the unknown on its own terms.

I am still turning over Hara Ko's note about the voice she heard in the corridor, the bad sleep. A small detail, perhaps, but sometimes those are the ones that unravel later. I will watch for anything that might have disturbed their quiet.
