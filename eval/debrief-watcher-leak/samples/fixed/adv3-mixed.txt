I watched them arrive on Cloud City, the initial approach already complicated. The Wing Guard had the docks locked down, a clear sign Varga had bought his way into a quiet space. I noted the crew’s choice to pay the bribe. It cleared their path quickly, avoiding any immediate violence or a public scene. Reyla Sun later reflected on the cost, saying she did not love what they became to make it cheap. It was a pragmatic decision, certainly, saving time and resources that a confrontation would have consumed. But it also meant accepting the corrupt system, becoming part of its operation, however briefly. The alternative would have been to fight their way through, a more direct challenge to the local order, but one that would have drawn unwanted attention and potentially more serious consequences at the outset.

I observed their attempts to connect with the Ugnaught administrators, Fyren and Krygg. That alliance is still forming, the full implications not yet clear. The information about Varga siphoning Tibanna gas for the Empire certainly changed the stakes. Later, at Refinery Platform 13, I watched them engage the Trandoshan mercenaries and the corrupt Ugnaught riggers. After the skirmish, they chose to cut the remaining hostiles loose. It was a swift end to the engagement, minimizing further conflict and allowing them to disengage quickly. This spared additional bloodshed, which some might see as a restraint. Yet, I also noted the argument that followed among the crew, lasting nearly an hour. Leaving such individuals to regroup always carries a risk, a potential loose end that could unravel later. A more decisive, perhaps more brutal, action would have secured the area completely, but at what internal cost to the crew, and what external cost in time and unwanted attention? The datapad recovered from the Trandoshan leader mentioned the *Shackles of Nizon* and Imperial deliveries; a detail I filed away.

Old Pike mentioned hearing a voice in the corridor, unplaced, dismissing it as nothing. I made a note of it. The sabacc tournament is the next stage, I understand. Lieutenant Armen Arandis and Lady Mirana Fioro are the key players there. The clock is ticking on that particular Imperial payment.

I am still turning over the implications of the *Shackles of Nizon* detail. I will watch how that information sits with them, and what it might push them to do, or not do, should it surface again.
