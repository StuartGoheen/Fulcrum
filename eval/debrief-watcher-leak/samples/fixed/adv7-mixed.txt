I watched them move through the lower sumps of Eriadu, a familiar kind of trapped. The Empire's presence was heavy, as expected. Their opening move, choosing to pay the bribe to clear the dock, was efficient. It bought them passage without violence, kept their hands clean of immediate conflict, and conserved whatever resources they might have been carrying. A pragmatic choice, certainly. But I marked Reyla Sun's words later, about not loving "what we became to make it cheap." It cost them something, then, beyond the credits. A small concession to what they likely oppose, perhaps. The alternative, a fight, would have declared their defiance more clearly, but at a price of time and exposure that might have been too high, too early.
