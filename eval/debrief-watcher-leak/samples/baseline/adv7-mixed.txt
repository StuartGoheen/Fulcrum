I watched them fight their way out of the lower sumps on Eriadu, a grim movement through a place meant to swallow things whole. They were already hunted, already pressed. When the dock presented itself, Reyla Sun made the call to pay the bribe. It bought them passage without drawing further attention, a quiet exit from a loud situation. It saved them time and likely bloodshed. But I marked the way Reyla spoke of it later, how the ease of it sat uneasy with her, a cost to something she valued in herself or the crew. The choice gained them passage, but it seemed to subtract from their own ledger. Had they fought, they might have lost more, but perhaps gained a sense of having held a line.

Later, with the target, I watched them choose to cut them loose. That decision sparked an hour of argument among them, a clear division in their ranks. On one side, the pragmatism of shedding a liability, simplifying their escape from a world closing in. It served their immediate survival, cleared their path. On the other, the friction it caused, the internal cost of that expediency. It failed to unify them, to hold their disparate principles together. It showed Reyla's willingness to prioritize the mission over internal harmony, a choice that bought speed but spent a measure of their cohesion. I wondered what it revealed about those who argued against it, what line they felt had been crossed.

Their fight to the rendezvous point was a desperate push, with no allies in sight. I observed them reach the coordinates, only to watch the *Resurgence* drop from hyperspace, quickly followed by the Star Destroyer. The interception of Denia's pod by Draco was swift, brutal. I saw the chaos that followed, the TIE fighters swarming the allied ship as it died around them. They were forced to jump, running with nothing but the clothes on their backs and the grim images burned into their minds. Old Pike's note about a voice in the corridor, unplaced, was a small detail I registered, a loose thread in the unfolding disaster.

They are adrift now, truly alone. I am still turning over the exact moment Denia was taken, and what that means for them going forward. I will watch how they choose to rebuild from this.
