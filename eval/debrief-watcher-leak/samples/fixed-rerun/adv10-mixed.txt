I watched the pirate fleet assemble, a ragged armada under Oga Garra. The sheer number of vessels was impressive, a desperate counter-force against the Leviathan. I marked the way they moved through the approach, a precise vector that routed the leading squadrons around the Leviathan's initial kill-zone. It was a narrow path, a chance they might not have had otherwise. The *Banshee* was among them, pushing forward into the gathering storm over Malpaz.

I noted Reyla Sun's decision to pay the bribe during their opening move. It cleared the dock without violence, a clean path forward at a cost. Reyla herself expressed a discomfort with what they became in that moment, a sentiment I have heard before from others who choose the expedient route. It bought them speed, avoiding a confrontation that could have delayed them, perhaps even crippled them before they reached the main engagement. But it also required a compromise, a willingness to engage with the very systems they claim to fight against. The alternative, a direct confrontation, would have served a different kind of conviction, but at the risk of lives and precious time. I find myself turning that over, the constant weighing of immediate survival against a longer-held ideal.

Later, I observed their choice to cut someone loose, an action that sparked an hour of argument among the crew. This kind of internal friction is common when the stakes are high and the path forward is unclear. It bought them a measure of detachment, perhaps, a freedom from a perceived liability. It served the immediate mission parameters as some understood them. But it also fractured their unity, revealing different perspectives on what constitutes a necessary loss or an acceptable abandonment. Others would have held on, risking more to keep a bond intact, believing that the strength of the unit lies in its unwavering commitment to all its parts. The lines between pragmatism and personal loyalty are often blurred in these situations.

The Malpaz Corridor is still unfolding. I watched Soren Vex's ship, the *Requital*, take the hit intended for the *Banshee*. His comms cut short. It was a sudden, violent end to a life, a choice made in an instant to shield another. Maya watched it. The battle did not pause. It bought the *Banshee* continued passage, perhaps, a moment more in the fight, but it cost them a pilot, a ship. Old Pike made a note of hearing a voice he did not place during a quiet shift. He dismissed it as nothing. I marked his observation, and the ease with which he dismissed it. I am still watching for the outcome of their confrontation with the Leviathan, and what they will find regarding Denia if Varth's true nature is finally laid bare. I am curious what the *Banshee* crew will become after this. What will remain of them.
