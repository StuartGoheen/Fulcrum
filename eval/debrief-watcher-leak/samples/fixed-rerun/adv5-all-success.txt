I watched the *Banshee* make planetfall on Malpaz, a world clearly under a heavy Imperial boot. The air tasted of industry and quiet desperation. My observations began immediately, marking their first significant choice at the dock. They chose to pay the bribe. It secured them passage without violence, a clean entry that avoided immediate complications. Kira Voss, however, later voiced a discomfort with what that transaction implied for them. A quick path, yes, but I noted it carried a quiet cost to her, a sense of something conceded. The alternative, a direct confrontation, would have undoubtedly drawn more attention and resources, but perhaps without that particular internal friction. I log it as a choice for efficiency over a more confrontational, possibly principled, stand. What it bought was time and discretion; what it cost was, for some, a piece of their self-perception.

Inside the research station, I watched them secure the drift algorithms. The data Varth needed is now complete. During the infiltration, I marked another choice: they opted to take their targets alive. This decision avoided unnecessary killing and kept their options open for information, a certain restraint. But it also led to an hour of argument among the crew, a visible fracture in their unity at a critical moment. Tarn Brec's note on a restless night and an unplaced voice in the corridor suggests the lingering impact of these close quarters and the tensions that arise. Had they chosen a swifter, more definitive action, that internal debate would have been avoided, though it would have introduced a different kind of burden, perhaps. I observe that this choice, while potentially preserving some moral ground, complicated their immediate operational cohesion.

I watched the insurrection spread across Malpaz. Their actions, intended for a single objective, stirred something in the oppressed population. The extraction became a scramble through a world in open revolt, a consequence they did not plan for. The algorithms are now with them, the last piece Varth required. I am still turning over the way their choices, made for specific, contained reasons, ripple outwards into something far larger and less controllable. I will be watching to see how this crew navigates the unintended consequences of their successes.
