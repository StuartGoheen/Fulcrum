I marked their arrival on Eriadu. Trapped, as I expected. The lower sumps offered little comfort, but also a kind of cover. I watched them make their opening move at the dock. Reyla Sun chose to pay the bribe. It was a pragmatic call, certainly. It cleared their path without a fight, avoiding immediate Imperial attention and preserving their limited resources. But I noted Reyla's own words later, the unease she carried about what that transaction made them. Old Pike, too, registered a subtle shift; he slept badly, heard things. What they gained in expediency, they may have lost in a small piece of themselves, or in the quiet trust that binds them. A quick exit, but at an unseen cost. 

Later, I observed another hard choice: to cut someone loose. The crew argued for an hour, I marked. That friction was palpable, even from a distance. This decision undoubtedly served their primary objective, whatever it was at that moment – to move faster, to reduce their risk profile, to maintain focus on the escape. It stripped away a liability, streamlining their path. Yet, the argument itself showed the internal cost. What it bought in efficiency, it spent in cohesion. It revealed a core willingness to make difficult sacrifices for the sake of the mission, but it also exposed a fault line, a disagreement about where that line should be drawn. 

I am still watching them fight their way to the rendezvous. That path remains incomplete. I saw the Resurgence drop out of hyperspace, a target for the Star Destroyer that followed it in. The TIE fighters swarmed. I saw Draco move to intercept Denia's escape pod, a calculated extraction. They watched the Resurgence die, I know. Watched everything taken from them. I saw them jump to hyperspace with nothing but the clothes on their backs and the knowledge of Varth's betrayal. 

I am curious to see what this crucible on Eriadu forges. Will these losses, these compromises, harden them into something unyielding, or will they splinter under the weight? I will be watching to see what lessons they carry forward from this particular kind of defeat.
