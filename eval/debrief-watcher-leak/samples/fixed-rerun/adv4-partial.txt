The initial briefing seemed straightforward enough, a search for ancient charts. I marked their first encounter with the local wildlife, a boar-wolf, as unremarkable, a common enough test of reflexes.

Their first significant choice came at the dock. They chose to pay the bribe. It was a pragmatic move, I noted, clearing their path without violence or unnecessary attention. It bought them immediate ease of passage, a clear path forward. Yet, I also observed the cost, as Vex Marrin later reflected, speaking of "what we became to make it cheap." It was a transaction that perhaps left a lingering taste, a small compromise to their own self-perception, even if it served the immediate objective.

Later, I saw them choose to "cut them loose." This decision spared them further direct involvement, avoiding a deeper entanglement that might have complicated their mission or exposed them to additional risk. It bought them distance, a clean break from a potential liability. However, I also noted the internal friction it generated; they argued for an hour after. That argument suggests the choice exacted a price in internal cohesion, leaving questions hanging between them. Hara Ko's note about sleeping badly and hearing a voice she couldn't place, though perhaps unrelated, speaks to an underlying tension, an unease that the environment or their choices might be stirring.

Now, I am watching the unfolding situation with the Ewok war party. Diplomacy is clearly required here, a different kind of negotiation than the dock bribe. I observe their cautious approach, the potential for either parley or confrontation. The outcome of this "meaningful decision" is still pending, and I am curious to see how they navigate this, what they prioritize when faced with an unfamiliar culture that holds the keys to their next step.

I am still turning over what the internal arguments and private reflections reveal about them. I will watch closely to see if these small, accumulating compromises erode their collective purpose, or if they forge something new from the tension.
