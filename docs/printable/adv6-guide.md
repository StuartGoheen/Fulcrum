# The Core of Corruption — GM Quick Reference

The heroes track Varth to Eriadu — Moff Tarkin's seat of power — and infiltrate his Citadel to learn the truth. Through the Glass Bridge, they witness Varth receive command of Leviathan from Tarkin himself. They must slice the Primary Data Center for Varth's transit orders while fighting Death Troopers and Draco's Apprentice, then escape through the acid rain of Eriadu's industrial hellscape.

## Marks (Destiny Footprints)

Schema: each mark is worth 1 footprint; tagged marks contribute to that destiny track. Crucible marks are GM-visible only.

### Tarkin's World (p1 / s1)
 — Reach Eriadu and navigate the most heavily Imperial world in the Outer Rim.

### The Citadel (p1 / s2)
 — Infiltrate Tarkin's Citadel — the seat of power for the entire Outer Rim.

### The Glass Bridge _[discovery]_ (p2 / s1)
 — Witness the truth through the Glass Bridge. Varth receives command of the Leviathan from Tarkin's hand. The betrayal is no longer a suspicion — it is a fact carried out of this room.

### The Primary Data Center _[discovery]_ (p2 / s2)
 — Slice into the PDC and extract Varth's transit orders. The clock is ticking.

### Walked the Citadel as if You Belonged _[ascendancy]_ (p2 / s2)
 — Move through Tarkin's command corridors with credentials borrowed, forged, or simply worn — and the Imperial command structure responds to a member of the crew as if they belonged in that corridor. A Moff's adjutant takes an order from you that they would not have taken from anyone outside the room. The Crime Lord road runs through every kind of room, including this one.

### The Apprentice (p2 / s3)
 — Face Draco's Apprentice in the corridors of the Citadel.

Paths:
- **End the Apprentice** _[destruction]_ — You put down a young Inquisitor before he becomes the next Draco. The Inquisitorius loses a piece on its board.
- **Spare and break him** _[corruption]_ — You leave him alive but turned — frightened, marked, his loyalty to the Inquisitorius cracked. The Empire keeps the man, but not what was inside him.

### The Hellscape (p3 / s1)
 — Escape through Eriadu's industrial acid rain. The planet itself is hostile.

### The Anonymous Message _[discovery]_ (p3 / s2)
 — On the quiet jump out of Eriadu, an unsigned comm comes through the Banshee's private channel. Someone in the Galactic Center is asking after the crew. They are not Imperial. The card from Maz's was not just a card. The crew has been watched longer than they knew.

### The Crucible (p3 / crucible) **[GM-only]**
 — The plan catastrophically fails. Improvise under fire to survive.
