# The Ebon Spire — GM Quick Reference

Varth has promised a bigger payday at Vel Shara in the Unknown Regions. To get there, the crew needs ancient hyperspace charts. Denia points them to the Ebon Spire — Revan's forward base on the Forest Moon of Endor — where Jedi holocrons with navigational data should be stored. But the Dead Forest is strong with the Dark Side, and the Spire's defenses don't care that the war ended millennia ago.

## Marks (Destiny Footprints)

Schema: each mark is worth 1 footprint; tagged marks contribute to that destiny track. Crucible marks are GM-visible only.

### The Dead Forest (p1 / s2)
 — Navigate the toxic, Dark Side-saturated forest to reach the Spire. The moon resists you.

### First Contact (p1 / s4)
 — The Ewok scouts hold the only safe path to the Spire. The forest watches what you choose.

Paths:
- **Lower weapons — parley** _[atonement]_ — You take the cost of patience. The Ewoks take you to their Storyteller. The forest grants you safe passage and you carry a name out of the dead grove.
- **Be named by the Storyteller — Hand of the Forest** _[ascendancy]_ — You go further than parley — you sit at the Storyteller's fire and accept the name the tribe gives you. From this day forward, the Ewok elders of Pzob recognize a member of the crew as a Hand of the Forest. The forest has a chair with your name on it.
- **Burn through them** _[destruction]_ — You clear the obstacle with overwhelming force. The Ewoks scatter. The forest turns hostile and the Spire takes its toll on you for it.

### Breaching the Spire (p2 / s1)
 — Find a way past the ancient defenses that still guard Revan's fortress.

### Echoes of the Dark _[atonement]_ (p2 / s2)
 — Confront what the Dark Side shows you inside the Spire. It knows your fears, and naming them is the cost of walking past them.

### The Holocron Vault _[discovery]_ (p2 / s3)
 — Locate the Jedi holocrons containing the hyperspace charts to Vel Shara. Open them and bring a Republic-era truth back into the light.

### The Guardian _[destruction]_ (p2 / s4)
 — End the ancient defender that has held the Spire's threshold for millennia. The last warden falls because of you.

### The Way Forward _[discovery]_ (p2 / s4)
 — Secure the navigational data and escape the Spire before it seals you in. The galaxy's least-known corridor is now in your hand.

### The Crucible (p3 / crucible) **[GM-only]**
 — The plan catastrophically fails. Improvise under fire to survive.
