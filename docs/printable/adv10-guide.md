# Jaws of the Leviathan — GM Quick Reference

The Maw drops out of hyperspace over Malpaz — the world the heroes liberated, now targeted for Tarkin's demonstration of the gravity well superweapon they helped Varth unlock. Its crescent arms open and the sky begins to distort as asteroids shift orbit. With Oga Garra's pirate fleet and whatever allies remain, the heroes race to board the Leviathan and disable the gravity well array before it buries the planet under a rain of kinetic impactors. The campaign ends where the betrayal began. If Denia was abandoned in Adventure 2, the Draco showdown reveals her alive in his custody — broken but surviving. The heroes discover that Varth told them to leave her so he could hand her to the Inquisition.

## Marks (Destiny Footprints)

Schema: each mark is worth 1 footprint; tagged marks contribute to that destiny track. Crucible marks are GM-visible only.

### Return to Malpaz (p1 / s1)
 — The world you liberated is now the target. The Leviathan arrives.

### The Fleet Assembles (p1 / s1)
 — Rally Oga Garra's pirates and whatever allies you've earned across the campaign.

### The Corridor's Cost _[atonement]_ (p1 / s2)
 — Someone in the fleet flies a missile that was meant for the Banshee. Soren Vex if he came. Brell if he stayed. The Banshee survives because someone else did not. The Atonement track grows.

### Boarding Action (p2 / s1)
 — Board the Leviathan while the fleet buys you time in the orbital battle.

### The Inquisitor's End _[destruction]_ (p2 / s2)
 — Draco stands between you and the bridge. You end him here. The Inquisitorius loses its piece on this board.

### The Reckoning _[discovery]_ (p2 / s3)
 — Face Varth on the empty command deck. He is barefoot. He is honest for the first time. You name the truth he has been carrying — and you carry it out of the room.

### The Gravity Well Array _[liberation]_ (p3 / s1)
 — Reach the projector core and disable it. Malpaz is no longer a target. The Leviathan no longer holds a sky hostage.

### The Leviathan Undone _[destruction]_ (p3 / s2)
 — The Empire's gravity-well dreadnought is a wreck. Three sectors of strategic doctrine collapse with it.

### The Routes Convergence (p3 / s3)
 — On the empty deck after the bridge falls, three patrons appear in person to ask for the same thing — the Vel Shara routes and the Leviathan's data. Bail Organa's Fulcrum handler. Luthen Rael himself. Quinlan Vos, revealing himself as the man who has been Cade Ryll. The crew chooses what becomes of the routes.

Paths:
- **Keep the routes — Enterprise** _[creation, ascendancy]_ — You walk away as the only crew that owns the most valuable trade asset in the Western Reaches. Creation built. Ascendancy claimed.
- **Give to Bail — routes-as-shelter** _[creation]_ — You hand the data to Bail's handler. Slow. Safe. Generational. The Path widens. You walk out lighter than you came in.
- **Give to Luthen — routes-as-weapons** _[liberation, destruction]_ — You hand the data to Vel's patron. Strike-and-fade. Three sectors broken in a year. The Empire bleeds at galactic scale.
- **Give to Vos — routes-as-sanctuary** _[rescue, creation]_ — You hand the data to Cade Ryll, who is and was Quinlan Vos. Force-sensitives sheltered. The hidden Path widened. The campaign began with a Jedi and ends with one.
- **Split the routes — each gets a partial map** _[ascendancy]_ — You couldn't choose, so you became the only one who can. Each patron leaves with less than they wanted. The crew walks out as the seam between three networks — the only chair in the galaxy with the full map.
- **Destroy the routes — no one holds this** _[atonement]_ — You burn the data in front of all three. No Empire, no Resistance, no Path. Some things should not exist. The Atonement is total.

### Carrying the Truth Out _[discovery]_ (p3 / s3)
 — Whoever leaves the Convergence with the routes also leaves with the truth — Varth, Tarkin, the Leviathan program, the names. The campaign's hidden ledger is now in someone's hands.

### The Crucible (p3 / crucible) **[GM-only]**
 — The plan catastrophically fails. Improvise under fire to survive.
