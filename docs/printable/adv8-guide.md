# The Iron Blockade — GM Quick Reference

The crew needs a fleet. The Leviathan is heading to Malpaz for a demonstration strike, and the people who rose up because of the crew's actions in Adventure 5 are the target. Hunted and battered, the crew arrives at Black Spire Outpost on Batuu — the one place in the galaxy where the Empire's reach is short and the local boss has the firepower to help. Oga Garra is transactional, not ideological. She'll assemble a pirate fleet because Leviathan is bad for business, but she'll collect on the debt.

## Marks (Destiny Footprints)

Schema: each mark is worth 1 footprint; tagged marks contribute to that destiny track. Crucible marks are GM-visible only.

### Black Spire Outpost (p1 / s1)
 — Land on Batuu under Maya's old smuggler credentials. The Empire's reach is short here.

### The Cantina at the End of the Reach (p1 / s2)
 — Sit across from Oga Garra. She runs the captains, the docks, and the only registry of who can run what through the Cordon. Whatever you walk out with, your standing in the Western Reaches now reaches her chair.

Paths:
- **Negotiate as equals — the partnership** _[ascendancy]_ — You pay her tax cleanly, you take her terms, you walk out as a partner. Black Spire is now a base the crew can return to. The Crime Lord road is real and it is shared with Oga.
- **Hostile takeover — Oga's chair** _[ascendancy]_ — You break her grip on Black Spire and you sit where she sat. The Western Reaches underworld answers to you now. You are the new Oga. The Toll path's price falls on the people who depended on her quiet rule.

### Three Patrons, One Decision (p1 / s2)
 — A woman from the front booth — Vel Sartha, the face that has been at the other end of every anonymous message since Eriadu — makes her patron's offer at the same hour Bail Organa's Fulcrum handler arrives with a competing one. You choose who you fly for. The patron's name comes later.

Paths:
- **Take Bail's offer — Fulcrum** _[creation]_ — You sign on with Bail's network. Slow. Safe. Generational. You are building a shelter that will outlast you.
- **Take her patron's offer — Axis** _[creation]_ — You sign on with Vel's patron. Strike-and-fade. Three sectors broken in a year. You are building a weapon that will cost what weapons cost.
- **Take both — run Axis while Bail thinks you're his** _[corruption]_ — You take both signatures on the same flimsi. Bail does not know about Vel. Vel knows about Bail. You make yourself the seam. The Toll arrives the day one of them finds out.

### The Captain (p1 / s3)
 — Pick the captain who flies the cordon run.

Paths:
- **Captain Maku — the professional** _[atonement]_ — Mon Calamari, Old Rebellion vintage. Quiet contract. Honest fee. You hire competence and get it.
- **Captain Brell — the easy yes** _[corruption]_ — Charming, cheap, and three amendments deep into a contract you should have read twice. You bought a betrayal you have not yet been billed for.
- **Captain Vex — the harder yes** _[atonement]_ — Maya's old partner, the man who sold her. You hire him with her at the ramp. The crew names what was broken and decides whether the fix is on the table.

### Load-Out (p1 / s3)
 — Move the cargo, the codes, and the kit aboard. The Banshee stays at Black Spire under Maya's authority.

### Breaking the Cordon _[destruction]_ (p2 / s1)
 — Run the gauntlet. The Imperial cordon over the Hosnian / Malpaz / Xala-Cermau seams is no longer a cordon when you are through it.

### The Jump to Lightspeed (p2 / s2)
 — Out of the cordon, into deep space. The pirate fleet is yours.

### The Crucible (p2 / crucible) **[GM-only]**
 — The plan catastrophically fails. Improvise under fire to survive.
