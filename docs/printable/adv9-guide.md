# The Point of No Return — GM Quick Reference

The heroes travel the Xala-Cermau hyperlane into Wild Space to assault Inquisitor Draco's stronghold on Xala. Master Denia is held there — if she survived. The rescue attempt ends in sacrifice, and the heroes leave the edge of known space carrying the cost. This adventure is structurally optional — players who choose not to pursue Denia proceed directly to the finale, but without whatever advantage Xala would have provided.

## Marks (Destiny Footprints)

Schema: each mark is worth 1 footprint; tagged marks contribute to that destiny track. Crucible marks are GM-visible only.

### Beyond the Charts (p1 / s1)
 — Travel the Xala-Cermau hyperlane into Wild Space. Past here, the galaxy ends.

### The Stronghold (p1 / s2)
 — Locate and infiltrate Draco's fortress on Xala.

### The Rescue (p1 / s2)
 — Find Denia. If you saved her in Adv2, this is the second time you pull her out — from a fate even she had stopped naming. If she was abandoned in Adv2, this is the toll-path: you recover what you let happen.

Paths:
- **Pull her out of Draco's hand** _[rescue]_ — You break her cell. You carry her out. The road that opened in Adv2 closes here, on her terms.
- **Recover what you let happen** _[atonement]_ — She is in Draco's keeping because Varth told you to leave her and you listened. Pulling her out now is not rescue — it is paying a debt you helped sign.

### The Inquisitor _[destruction]_ (p2 / s1)
 — End Draco on his own ground. The Inquisitorius loses a piece of its board.

### The Cost _[atonement]_ (p2 / s1)
 — Someone pays the price of the rescue. The Atonement track grows by the weight of who you spent.

### Carrying It Home _[discovery]_ (p2 / s2)
 — Leave the edge of known space. What you bring back changes the final fight — and what you saw of Draco's stronghold is its own kind of evidence.

### The Last Briefing (p2 / s2)
 — The Resistance staging dock. Sixteen ships. Twenty thousand crew. The Leviathan run is at first sun.

### The Crucible (p2 / crucible) **[GM-only]**
 — The plan catastrophically fails. Improvise under fire to survive.
